Our story

Built from the ground up in Newcastle upon Tyne.

CPB Distribution started trading in September 2024 with a simple approach: buy well-known branded stock smartly, move it on efficiently, and reinvest every bit of profit back into growing the business.

Who we are

A lean, focused wholesale distribution operation

CPB Distribution, a trading name of CPBTrade LTD, is a UK-based wholesale distribution business operating out of Newcastle upon Tyne in the North East of England. We buy and distribute products across a wide range of categories and brands, with fulfilment and logistics handled through our prep centre in Brighton so we can focus on sourcing and growth.

Since launching in September 2024, the business has grown every single quarter. That growth hasn't come from external funding — it's come from disciplined reinvestment. Profit made on sales goes straight back into buying more stock and building working capital, compounding growth quarter after quarter.
